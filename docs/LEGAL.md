# Responsible use / Uso responsable

- **Analyze technique, rebuild with your own assets.** Rhythm, framing, grading and movement are
  techniques. Film footage, music tracks, logos and people's likeness belong to their owners.
- **Reference films**: `index-ref` needs a copy you are entitled to use. Indexes store only
  perceptual hashes, not images. Do not commit films, keyframes or indexes of copyrighted works to
  public repositories (`runs/` and `refs/` are git-ignored).
- **Downloading**: respect each platform's terms of service when using `yt-dlp`.
- **People**: identify public performers only in the context of a verified film; never identify
  private individuals from their face.
- This is not legal advice. Fair use / fair dealing rules differ by country — check yours.

---

- **Analiza la técnica, reconstruye con tus propios recursos.** Ritmo, encuadre, color y movimiento
  son técnica; el metraje, la música, las marcas y la imagen de las personas tienen dueño.
- `index-ref` requiere una copia que tengas derecho a usar. No subas películas, keyframes ni índices
  de obras con copyright a repos públicos.
- Respeta los términos de cada plataforma al descargar. No identifiques a personas privadas.
- Esto no es asesoría legal.
